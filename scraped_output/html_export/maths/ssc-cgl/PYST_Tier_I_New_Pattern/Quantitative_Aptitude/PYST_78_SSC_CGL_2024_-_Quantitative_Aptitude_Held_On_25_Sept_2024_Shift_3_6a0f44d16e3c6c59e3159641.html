Circular Motion (Same Direction)

When two bodies move in the same direction on a circular track, the time taken to meet for the first time anywhere on the track is calculated as Track Length ÷ (Speed1 − Speed2).

Circular Motion (Opposite Direction)

\n

When two bodies move in opposite directions, their relative speed increases. The time taken to meet for the first time is Track Length ÷ (Speed1 + Speed2).
