Additional Information

\n

Congruence Criteria

\n

Two triangles are strictly congruent if they satisfy SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, or RHS congruency conditions.

\n

CPCT Theorem

\n

CPCT denotes 'Corresponding Parts of Congruent Triangles'. It establishes that if two triangles are congruent, all their equivalent angles and sides are identical.

\n

Angle Sum Property

\n

The total sum of interior angles in any triangle is exactly 180°. This helps determine the third angle if two are known (∠A = 180° − ∠B − ∠C).

\n

Similar vs Congruent

\n

Congruent triangles have an identical shape and exact size, whereas similar triangles possess the identical shape but may differ proportionally in size.
